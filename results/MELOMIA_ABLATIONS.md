# MeLoMIA ablations

Generated by `python scripts/melomia_ablations.py table`; arms are defined in that script.
Per-run rows: `results/melomia_ablations.csv`.  Per-arm rows (tag, cross-validated AUC, where the protocol record came from): `results/melomia_ablations_arms.csv`.

**Protocol, identical in every arm.** Black box. 5 generators × splits 1–5 of each cohort (the slide-table targets, fingerprints checked). Classifier search: Optuna, 60 trials per classifier, ensemble of xgb, rf, lgbm, cat, mlp. Feature grids: 600 frozen draws × 15 timesteps (ND), 50 draws × 6 temperatures (CVAE). Shadow k is the same model in every arm. Cells are means over 5 splits; "(n)" marks fewer. "Cross-validated AUC" is the attacker's own estimate on shadows, never an attack result.
